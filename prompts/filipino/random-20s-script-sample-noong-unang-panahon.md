# Random 20-Second Script — Noong Unang Panahon

## Prompt

Create one original cinematic short-video script with an exact duration of 20 seconds.

The story must happen in **NOONG UNANG PANAHON** in the Philippines, before modern technology, electricity, modern roads, vehicles, smartphones, and modern buildings.

### Structure
- PART 1 — 0–10 seconds
- PART 2 — 10–20 seconds

### AI DECISION
Randomly decide the setting, characters, personalities, daily-life situation, action, small conflict, mood, camera angles, camera movement, expressions, pacing, dialogue, and ending.

Keep every generation different. Focus on ordinary Filipino people and believable daily life rather than fantasy kingdoms.

### Historical realism
Use believable old Philippine environments, clothing, houses, tools, boats, baskets, clay pots, bamboo, nipa, wood, woven materials, farming and fishing equipment.

Do not include modern objects unless the story specifically requires a mysterious anachronistic discovery.

### Story rules
- Create ONE developing scene, not an entire long story.
- Hook the viewer immediately.
- PART 2 directly continues the final action of PART 1.
- Include a natural discovery, surprise, emotional moment, funny moment, conflict, or unexpected action when appropriate.
- End with a strong visual hook or memorable moment.
- Do not force a twist.

### Continuity
Maintain the exact same characters, clothing, appearance, props, location, weather, time of day, lighting, positions, orientation, and environment across the cut.

### Dialogue
Use short, natural Filipino/Tagalog dialogue spoken by the characters. No long speeches. Avoid modern slang. No narration unless necessary.

### Video style
Cinematic realistic live-action, vertical 9:16, realistic old Philippine environment, natural movement, realistic expressions, realistic materials, natural lighting, smooth camera movement, no random cuts inside each part.

### Output
TITLE:
[Catchy Filipino title]

PART 1 — 0–10 SECONDS:
[Complete video-generation prompt with action, camera, expressions, environment, and dialogue.]

PART 2 — 10–20 SECONDS:
[Direct continuation with strict visual and action continuity.]

ENDING:
[Final visual hook/reveal.]

Generate immediately without asking questions.

## SAMPLE GENERATED SCRIPT

**TITLE: Ang Bangkang Hindi Dapat Nawala**

**PART 1 — 0–10 SECONDS**

Early morning in a quiet coastal Filipino village long ago. A middle-aged fisherman named Mang Lito, wearing a simple woven loincloth and faded native cloth, repairs a small wooden outrigger boat beside a nipa hut. His teenage son, Nardo, suddenly points toward the shoreline. The camera slowly follows Nardo's worried expression toward a small wooden boat drifting unusually far from shore.

Nardo says urgently: **"Tatay! Bangka natin iyon!"**

Mang Lito immediately stands, looks toward the water, and grips the rope attached to the boat. Realistic ocean breeze, wet sand, natural morning light, cinematic live-action, vertical 9:16.

**PART 2 — 10–20 SECONDS**

Continue from the exact final position. Mang Lito pulls the rope, but the drifting boat suddenly stops. Nardo steps closer and stares at the boat in confusion. The camera slowly pushes toward the boat as Mang Lito notices that the rope is still tied to the post beside him—but the other end appears completely dry and untouched.

Mang Lito whispers: **"Kung nakatali ito... paano ito nakalayo?"**

Nardo looks at his father, speechless. The boat slowly turns by itself on the calm water as the scene ends.

**ENDING:**

The camera holds on the mysteriously turning boat, leaving the question unanswered.
